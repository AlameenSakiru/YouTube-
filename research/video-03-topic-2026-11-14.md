## The topic: Superior Shoal

A 20-square-mile underwater mountain of ancient lava in the middle of Lake Superior, 50 miles north of Copper Harbor, Michigan. Its top is only 21 feet below the surface, in an otherwise deep part of the lake. Fishermen knew of it for generations, but it was only officially charted in 1929. It has been suggested as the cause of the loss of the French minesweepers Inkerman and Cerisoles (November 1918), the disappearance of the SS Bannockburn (1902) and even the Edmund Fitzgerald (1975). (Wikipedia, "Superior Shoal", citing the US Lake Survey and shipwreck histories; to be confirmed against primary sources while scripting.)
