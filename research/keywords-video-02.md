## What already ranks for "lake erie seiche"

Short news clips: WXYZ Detroit (about 1.4 million views), WTVG Toledo (about 259,000), SkyView Media (about 215,000), and local stations with 1 to 3 minutes of footage. There is no full documentary on the 1844 Buffalo seiche, which is the gap video 2 fills.

## What this can and cannot do

Tags and keywords tell YouTube what the video is about. They do not make it push a video. YouTube's own help pages say tags play a minimal role, and that the title, thumbnail and description matter most. Whether it spreads depends on how many people click and how long they watch.
