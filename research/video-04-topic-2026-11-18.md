## The topic

The salt mines under the Great Lakes: Cargill's Cleveland mine under Lake Erie, Morton's Fairport Harbor mine, the Detroit salt mine under the city (video 1's end card promised "the city under Detroit"), and Goderich under Lake Huron. The question that holds the video together: what keeps the lake out, and what happens when water does get into a salt mine. Two real answers: the Retsof mine in New York (1994, the largest salt mine in the Western Hemisphere, lost to flooding after a roof collapse) and Lake Peigneur in Louisiana (1980, a drilling rig punctured a salt mine and the lake drained into it).

All depths, sizes, dates and incidents to be checked against primary sources before scripting. Nothing from the "shouldn't exist" videos is used unless a primary source confirms it.
