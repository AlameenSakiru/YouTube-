## 3. Published studies

- Buffer's study of about 1.8 million videos put Sunday around 10 am local time as the best slot for long videos, with Wednesday and Thursday weakest.
- RecurPost (more than 2 million videos) and SocialPilot (301,000 videos) favour weekday afternoons, about noon to 4 pm.
- PostEverywhere (9,500+ videos) found Sunday 8 to 11 am best and the hour mattering more than the day.
- YouTube's own help pages give no best time. They point creators to Studio's "When your viewers are on YouTube" report (Analytics, Audience), which appears once a channel has enough viewers.

The studies disagree on the day but agree on the method: publish a few hours before your own audience's evening peak, so YouTube has time to test the video before most viewers are online.

## Decision

- **Weekdays:** publish at **19:00 GMT** (2 pm Eastern, 1 pm Central). That gives YouTube about four hours of testing before our 6 pm Eastern peak.
- **Weekends:** publish at **15:00 GMT** (10 am Eastern), which fits the largest study's Sunday-morning finding and leaves the whole day plus the evening peak.
- **Video 4:** Sunday 23 November, 15:00 GMT. Thursday 26 November is US Thanksgiving, and the long weekend is good viewing time for long documentaries.
- Recheck this once Studio's "When your viewers are on YouTube" card appears for the channel.
