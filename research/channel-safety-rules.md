# Channel safety rules (long-term)

Written 26 November 2026, after the user asked how to avoid YouTube flagging the channel for repetitive or template content. Check every new video against these before scripting.

## What YouTube's rules say (from reporting on the policy; the official page could not be opened from the build machine)

- The YouTube Partner Program's "repetitious content" rule was renamed "inauthentic content" on 15 July 2025 (one unconfirmed source says it was renamed again in 2026 to "Generic or Repetitive Content"). It covers mass-produced or repetitive videos, near-identical videos made from one template, and videos only slightly different from each other.
- It is a monetization rule: breaking it keeps the whole channel out of the Partner Program or removes it. Strikes and channel removal come from the separate spam rules (mass-produced or scraped content, misleading metadata).
- The same intro and outro are allowed if the bulk of each video is different. AI-assisted videos with real value can be monetized; realistic AI content must be disclosed.
- At 1,000 subscribers a human reviewer looks at the channel as a whole.

## Rules for this channel

1. **Vary the format.** No more than two videos in a row in the same format. Videos 4 and 5 are both "We Drained…", so video 6 uses a different format (an investigation like video 3, a single-ship story, a mystery profile).
2. **Write fresh calls to action for each video.** No stock subscribe/like/comment lines copied from video to video; each ask is written for that story.
3. **Every video is its own researched story,** with sources in the description and a fact-check table in its script notes.
4. **Change the look a little each time** (graphics set, colours, title cards), as videos 3, 4 and 5 already do.
5. **Reuse our own images sparingly** (about 10% of shots or less), only where it is genuinely the same ship, place or moment. Never reuse other people's footage or images.
6. **Honest metadata.** Tags only for what is in the video; no copied descriptions; hypothetical titles ("We Drained…") explained in the first minute.
7. **Always tick "Altered or synthetic content: Yes"** while the pictures are AI illustrations, and say so in the description.
8. **Keep the human side visible:** reply to comments, and bring viewers' knowledge into scripts (with permission when naming anyone).
9. **Consider a distinct narrator voice** (a custom voice or the user's own) instead of a widely used stock voice, before the Partner Program application.
10. **Never** work around YouTube's bot checks, buy views or subscribers, or use automated engagement.
