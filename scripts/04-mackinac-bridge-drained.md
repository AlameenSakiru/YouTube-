## How the script is built

It follows the winning "We Drained" videos: the water drops level by level, and something new appears at each depth. The depth is on screen the whole time.

| Depth | What appears | Section |
|---|---|---|
| 10–30 ft | The Rock Maze off Mackinac Island; the shoreline and islands grow | The drain begins |
| 35 ft | The top of the Cedarville's hull | Thirty-five feet |
| 65 ft | Line 5 comes out of the lake bed | Sixty-five feet |
| (100 ft high) | Mackinac Falls, a drowned waterfall | The waterfall |
| 70–145 ft | The shipwreck preserve; the whole Cedarville at 110 ft | The graveyard |
| 210 ft | The bridge tower foundations (each tower over 760 ft from base to top); the five workers | Two hundred and ten feet |
| 250–270 ft | Under mid-bridge; Line 5's lowest point | Two hundred and seventy feet |
| 295 ft | The bottom: the canyon of an ancient river | The river |

Open loops that keep people watching: "at the very bottom is something that could spread oil along hundreds of miles of shoreline" (0:45, paid off at about 9:30), "how does a waterfall end up a hundred feet underwater? The answer is at the very bottom" (about 5:00, paid off at about 11:00), and "the deeper we go, the more there is to say about these two pipes" (about 4:00).

Rechecked on 21 November: the first version opened section 2 with a block of background (the two lakes, the currents, the bridge's numbers) from about 1:15 to 2:35, the same pattern that lost half of video 2's viewers. The drain now starts straight after the subscribe ask, the bridge's numbers became a reveal at the towers, and "Michigan and Huron are one lake" moved to the pipeline section, where it explains why a leak would spread so far.

Honesty: the title is the genre's hypothetical format. At 0:30 the narrator says "We can't really, of course. But lake-bed maps, sonar and divers have shown us what's down there."
