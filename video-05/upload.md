The Edmund Fitzgerald went down just 17 miles from Whitefish Point, at the edge of the most dangerous water on Lake Superior: the Graveyard of the Great Lakes, where at least 240 ships have been lost. We drain it a few feet at a time and watch the wrecks appear: the Saturn, the Myron and her captain who survived 20 hours on the roof of his pilothouse, the Comet and her 70 tons of silver ore, the Superior City, and at the very bottom, 530 feet down, the Edmund Fitzgerald. Three things have been sinking ships here for 200 years: the storm, the dark and the cold. On the night of November 10, 1975, all three were waiting for her. And there may have been a fourth: the ship herself.
