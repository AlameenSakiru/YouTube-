## What to upload

### Images

- **Shape:** wide (16:9). Choose the largest size Flow offers.
- **Type:** PNG or JPG.
- **Name:** the image number from [image-prompts.docx](image-prompts.docx), three digits: `001.png` to `073.png`. Thumbnails are `thumb-1.png` to `thumb-3.png`.
- **Extra versions:** if you make two versions of an image, name the second `004b.png`. I will keep the better one.
- **Folder:** `video-01/images/`

You do not have to make every shot. If one will not come out right, skip it and I will cover the gap with a neighbouring image or a map.

Short video clips from Flow are welcome too. Name them the same way: `015.mp4`.

### Voiceover

- **Record each section of the script as its own file.** There are 12 sections. Name them `01.mp3` to `12.mp3`. This lets me line the pictures up with your words accurately.
- **Type:** MP3 or M4A. Do not upload WAV; the files are too large for GitHub.
- **Folder:** `video-01/voice/`
- Record in a quiet room, close to the microphone. Read at a steady pace. Leave one second of silence at the start and end of each file.

### Music (optional)

One calm instrumental track from the YouTube Audio Library, as `video-01/music/music.mp3`. I will keep it quiet under the voice.

## How to upload on GitHub

1. Open the repository on github.com.
2. Go into the `video-01` folder, then `images` (or `voice`).
3. Click **Add file → Upload files**.
4. Drag your files in. Each file must be under 25 MB.
5. Click **Commit changes**.
6. Tell me in the chat that the files are up.

## What you get back

- A finished video: 1920 by 1080, 30 frames a second, sound levelled for YouTube.
- A subtitle file.
- The thumbnail.

I will first make a 30-second sample from the opening, so you can check the look before I edit the whole video. A full-length video is a large file, and the sample also tests that I can get a file that size to you.

## Three rules for AI images

1. **Tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" box when you upload.** The images show a real event from 1954 but are not real photographs.
2. **I will put a small "illustration" label on the AI images of 1954** and say so in the description. Viewers in this niche care about this. One successful channel in the research advertises "no AI" in its titles.
3. **Check that your Flow plan allows the images to be used in monetized YouTube videos.**

Do not generate fake newspaper pages, fake documents, or the faces of real named people.
