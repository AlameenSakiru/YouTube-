## What we change, by what the numbers show

Change one thing at a time and wait 48 hours before judging it, so we know what worked. Every change goes in the log below.

| What we see | What it means | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Views per hour rising; "Browse features" and "Suggested videos" growing in traffic sources | YouTube is pushing it | Change nothing on video 1. Finish the follow-up video as fast as possible |
| Click rate under 4% after at least 1,000 impressions | The thumbnail or title is not making people click | Thumbnail first: switch to thumb-b or thumb-c, or run all three in Studio's Test & compare. If still low 48 hours later, change the title (backups below) |
| Under 65% still watching at 30 seconds | The opening does not deliver what the thumbnail and title promised, or starts slowly | Find the exact second of the drop on the retention graph. Fix it in the next videos' openings; on video 1, Studio's Editor can trim the start if the drop is in the first seconds |
| Average view duration under about 6 minutes (40%) | The middle loses people | Find the steep drops on the retention graph and what is on screen at each one. Cut that kind of section from the next scripts. Studio's Editor can cut a section out of video 1 if one part is clearly slow |
| Very few impressions | YouTube has not tested it widely yet. This is normal for a channel's first video | Keep publishing on schedule. Every new video gives YouTube another chance to find the audience |
| Search terms we did not expect, that the video does answer | People are finding it in search | Add those words to the description in a natural sentence |

### What not to do

- Do not buy views, subscribers or likes, or join "sub for sub" groups. It breaks YouTube's fake engagement policy and can cost the channel its monetization.
- Do not change a video that is working.
