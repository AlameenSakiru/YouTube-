# Video 1 shot list

80 shots for the script in [scripts/01-lake-michigan-tsunami.md](../scripts/01-lake-michigan-tsunami.md). Each picture stays on screen for about seven seconds.

- **FLOW** shots are yours to generate: 46 images.
- **ME** shots are maps, diagrams and text cards that I make: 34 shots.

## How to write each prompt

Paste the style line first, then the shot description.

**Style A, for 1954 scenes:**

> Black-and-white documentary photograph, 1954, Chicago lakefront on Lake Michigan, 35mm film grain, soft natural light, wide shot, realistic, no text, no watermark, 16:9.

**Style B, for present-day scenes:**

> Colour documentary photograph, present day, Lake Michigan, natural light, wide shot, realistic, no text, no watermark, 16:9.

Keep people small and seen from behind or from a distance. Do not show anyone drowning or injured.
