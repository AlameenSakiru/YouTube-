# Video 1 shot list

Shots for the final script in [scripts/01-lake-michigan-tsunami.md](../scripts/01-lake-michigan-tsunami.md). The section numbers match the script. Each picture stays on screen for about eight seconds.

- **FLOW** shots are yours to generate.
- **ME** shots are maps, diagrams and text cards that I make.

The totals are at the bottom of this file.

## How to write each prompt

Paste the style line first, then the shot description.

**Style A, for 1954 scenes:**

> Black-and-white documentary photograph, 1954, Chicago lakefront on Lake Michigan, 35mm film grain, soft natural light, wide shot, realistic, no text, no watermark, 16:9.

**Style B, for present-day scenes:**

> Colour documentary photograph, present day, Lake Michigan, natural light, wide shot, realistic, no text, no watermark, 16:9.

Rules for every image:

- Keep people small, or seen from behind or from a distance.
- Do not show anyone drowning or injured.
- Do not try to make a real named person's face.
- No readable signs, newspapers or documents.
