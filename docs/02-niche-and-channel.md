# Niche and channel setup

## The niche: business and money stories

Faceless documentaries about how companies and people made or lost fortunes: collapses, scams, bad decisions, and how businesses really make money.

You asked for three things. This niche is the best fit I know for all three together.

| What you asked for | Why this niche fits |
|---|---|
| High RPM | Finance and business advertisers (banks, investing apps, software) pay some of the highest ad rates on YouTube. Rates depend mostly on where your viewers live, so make videos in English for viewers in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. |
| A first video that can go viral | A story about a famous company failing interests almost anyone, not only finance fans. Tutorials and investing advice have high RPM but a much smaller audience. |
| Something big long term | The stories never run out, old videos keep getting views for years, and the audience attracts sponsors once you grow. |

It also suits a faceless channel: voiceover, stock footage, screenshots of news articles, charts and simple motion graphics are the normal style.

**The cost:** this niche is crowded with good channels. You will not beat them on famous stories told the same way. You win with a sharper angle, a fresher story, or a better title and thumbnail. The video briefs are built around that.

RPM figures you see online are estimates and vary a lot by season. Ad rates are usually highest in November and December and drop in January.

## Pick one angle and stay on it

A new channel grows faster when every video is clearly the same kind of thing. Choose one:

1. **Collapses (recommended):** "How [company] lost $X billion". The strongest hook and the easiest titles.
2. **Hidden business models:** "How [thing everyone uses] actually makes money".
3. **Scams and frauds:** "The $X billion lie". Very viral, but needs the most careful fact-checking.

Start with collapses. After six videos, look at your numbers and adjust.

## Channel setup checklist

- [ ] Create a new Google account used only for the channel
- [ ] Turn on 2-step verification (required for monetization)
- [ ] Create the channel and choose a handle
- [ ] Verify your phone number in YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility (unlocks custom thumbnails and videos over 15 minutes)
- [ ] Set country and language; set the default audience to "not made for kids"
- [ ] Add a simple logo and banner
- [ ] Write the channel description (template below)

## Name ideas

Check each on YouTube first and pick one nobody is using.

- Red Ink Stories
- The Write-Off
- Paper Losses
- Burn Rate Stories
- After the Hype

A good name is short, easy to spell, and hints at business or money.

## Channel description template

> Stories of how big companies and big money went wrong. Every video explains one collapse, scandal or bad bet: what happened, why, and what it cost. New video every three days.

## Tools for a faceless channel

All have free plans or free alternatives.

| Job | Tool |
|---|---|
| Research | News articles, court filings, company annual reports, Wikipedia for the outline (never as the only source) |
| Script | Google Docs |
| Voiceover | Your own voice with a phone and a quiet room is best. If you use an AI voice, pick one natural voice and keep it. |
| Editing | CapCut or DaVinci Resolve |
| Footage | Pexels and Pixabay (free stock video), your own screen recordings, simple charts |
| Thumbnails | Canva or Photopea |
| Music | YouTube Audio Library |

Only use footage, images and music you have the right to use. Copyright claims can block monetization.
