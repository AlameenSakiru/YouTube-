# Channel art: logo and banner

Prompts for Google Flow. Image generators often misspell words, so neither prompt asks for text. I add the channel name to the banner afterwards.

## YouTube's sizes

| | Size | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Banner | 2560 × 1440 recommended (minimum 2048 × 1152), 16:9, under 6 MB | Only a strip in the middle shows on every device: 1546 × 423 on a 2560 × 1440 canvas. TVs show the whole image; phones show only the middle strip |
| Logo (profile picture) | 800 × 800, square | Shown as a small circle, so the corners are cut off |

These figures are from third-party guides (2026), not YouTube's own help pages.

## Banner prompt

Flow settings: **16:9**, highest resolution.

> Wide panoramic black-and-white photograph of a calm Great Lakes shoreline in soft early-morning light. A long, low concrete breakwater runs in from the right, with a small lighthouse at its end standing just right of the centre of the frame. Everything important sits in a narrow horizontal band across the middle of the image: the flat horizon, the breakwater and the lighthouse. The upper third is plain, softly clouded sky and the lower third is calm, dark, open water, so the picture still works when the top, bottom and sides are cut off. The centre and left of that middle band are open water and sky, leaving clear empty space for a title. Shot as a black-and-white photograph on 35mm film, slightly grainy, with soft natural contrast and a quiet, mysterious mood. A clean image with plain, unmarked surfaces and no lettering anywhere. Cinematic 16:9 wide frame.

Why it is written this way: it puts the lighthouse inside the strip every device shows, keeps the top and bottom plain because phones crop them, and leaves empty space for the channel name.

## Logo prompt

Flow settings: **1:1**, highest resolution.

> A minimalist flat logo icon for a documentary channel about the Great Lakes: a simple warm-white lighthouse silhouette standing above one bold amber wave, centred on a solid deep navy background. The whole symbol sits inside the middle 70 percent of the square, with generous empty space around it, because the corners will be cut off by a circle. Thick, bold shapes and clean edges that stay clear when shown very small. Flat solid colours only: deep navy (#0D1419), warm amber (#F0A43A) and warm white (#EDE8DF), matching a dark documentary style. A clean design without any letters or words. Square 1:1 frame.

Why it is written this way: the logo is usually seen at about the size of a fingernail, so it needs one bold symbol, three flat colours and nothing near the corners. The colours match the maps and cards in the videos.
