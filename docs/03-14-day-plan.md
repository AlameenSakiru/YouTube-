# The 14-day plan

One video every three days, four videos published by day 13. Plan for 3–5 hours of work a day. If you have less time, keep the order and stretch the days.

## Days 1–4: set up and publish video 1

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| **1** | Set up the channel (checklist in [02-niche-and-channel.md](02-niche-and-channel.md)). Find 15 channels in the niche. For each, note the videos with far more views than the channel's usual. Those topics and titles are your guide. |
| **2** | Choose the topic for video 1 from [04-video-briefs.md](04-video-briefs.md). Research it from at least three reliable sources. Write the script (1,500–2,000 words). |
| **3** | Record the voiceover. Edit the video. Make three thumbnail versions and pick the clearest. |
| **4** | **Publish video 1.** Reply to every comment. Start researching video 2. |

## Days 5–7: video 2

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| **5** | Write the script for video 2. Record the voiceover. |
| **6** | Edit video 2. Make the thumbnail and title. |
| **7** | **Publish video 2.** Check video 1 in YouTube Studio (see "What to check" below). |

## Days 8–10: video 3

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| **8** | Write the script for video 3, using what you learned from video 1's numbers. Record the voiceover. |
| **9** | Edit video 3. Make the thumbnail and title. |
| **10** | **Publish video 3.** If video 1 or 2 has a low click rate, change its title or thumbnail. |

## Days 11–14: video 4 and review

| Day | Tasks |
|---|---|
| **11** | Write the script for video 4. Record the voiceover. |
| **12** | Edit video 4. Make the thumbnail and title. |
| **13** | **Publish video 4.** |
| **14** | Review all four videos. Decide what to make more of. Plan the next ten topics. |

## Every day

- Log subscribers and watch hours in the tracker.
- Reply to comments within the first hours after publishing.
- Watch one top video in the niche and write down its first 30 seconds word for word. This teaches hooks faster than anything else.

## What to check in YouTube Studio

Look at each video two to three days after publishing.

| Number | Where | Aim for | If it is low |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impressions click-through rate | Analytics → Reach | 4–6% or more | Change the thumbnail or title |
| Average view duration | Analytics → Engagement | 40% of the video length or more | Tighten the script; cut slow parts |
| Viewers still watching at 30 seconds | Retention graph | 65% or more | Rewrite the opening |

These are rough benchmarks, not rules from YouTube. Compare your videos with each other more than with the benchmark.

## Day 14 review

Answer these in writing:

1. Which video got the most views? What was different about its topic, title or thumbnail?
2. Which video held viewers longest? What did its opening do?
3. Where in each video did viewers leave?

Then make the next three videos as close as you can to the best performer: same kind of topic, same title style, same pacing.

## After day 14

- Keep publishing every three days. That is about 25 videos before 1 February 2027.
- When one video takes off, publish a follow-up on a related story within days.
- Apply for the Partner Program the day you pass 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours. Aim for early January at the latest.
